AP = Action points, how much stuff a unit can do per turn
HP = Hit points, how much damage a unit can take
DMG = Damage, the damage that a unit potentially serves
Armour = Flat damage reduction
Base movement cost (BMC) = The base cost for a unit to move a tile
Range = Max range
