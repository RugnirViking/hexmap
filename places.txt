Place:	Serenia
	IniPop: 100000
	IniIndustry: Flour, bread, beer
	Description: Birdsong. You see blue. Perhaps this is what a merchant-admiral sees,
	when they lead their armada across the grand oceans. Unlike those stories, you see no waves, hear no monsters,
	and feel no unease. Though you know that not all skies are alike. As you sit up, you wonder if you are still alive.
	Pinching your arm, you truly realise why Serenia was named such. A sea of gold fills your vision -
	though its value only reaches that of gold only in the most dire circumstances.
	Wheat fields, nearly as far as you can see. As you do the rest of your morning rituals,
	preparing for the final leg of the journey to Serenia,
	you thank the Gods of trade for not just blessing you with this experience,
	but also for ensuring that you arrived just before the harvest.
	You begin thinking of which places would be the best for you to ferry the wheat to, which goods they offer there,
	how long it takes, and by the time you reach the city gates, you have a few trade routes in mind.
	
Place: Dwaron's Hold
	IniPop: 1000000
	IniIndustry:
		Dwarven deep iron mining
		Dwarven smeltery
		Dwarven armourer
		Dwarven smithy
		Dwarven machinist
		Dwarven engineering guild
	Description:
		Established millennia ago, by Dwaron, one of the oldest dwarves in recorded history, this hold is among the
		few Dwarven ancient high holds, said to be the origins of Dwarven existence, which itself is a mystery.
		Dwaron's Hold is the centre of all Dwarves for thousands of kilometres, an official and unofficial capital for
		millions of Dwarves, where the most ambitious or prominent among them come to meet. 
		The hold, the Dwarven word for their stronghold cities, can be described as a labyrinth, anthill, citadel, and much else;
		every description would be true for some. It has naturally sprawled for millennia, but has also been rebuilt,
		restructured, and redistricted many times. THis is not just to ensure efficiency for the many goods which need to be
		transported all throughout the hold, but also to ensure that at least the greenest dwarflings have a chance at
		navigating this fortified metropolis.
		Foreign dignitaries, traders, and tourists are generally not prioritised. Some say that the council of Dwaron's Hold
		intentionally let this consideration slip, and the outsiders which do not give up in whatever endeavour brought them
		here, will be regarded with more respect, having shown a level of resilience, or stubborness, which Dwarves respect.
