# This document will contain all trade goods in the game
# It also contains more information about the trade goods, such as their traits.
# Full trait list, and explanation:
# -	Basic:
	Basic goods are at the first stage of production trees, assigned to all goods which are extracted from farms,
	mines, etc. The price of this good will determine the prices of more advanced goods in the area, but huge fluctuations
	in its availability will often lead to the destruction of livelihoods, famines, and other unpleasant events.
	Then again, great crises may lead to even greater profits...
# -	Food: 
	Food goods are edible for most chintabeings. A minimum local production or import of goods with this trait,
	are necessary to ensure that this location does not starve.
# -	Grain:
	Grain-type goods are grains, which can be important for different reasons,
	one of which include the desire for some cities to have a varied diet.
# -	Agriculture:
	Agricultural goods refers to goods which are the results of agriculture
# -	Crafts:
	Crafts goods refer to goods which are used in, or part of, a significant amount of production chains which involve
	crafting something, whether it is for handicraft, tools for farming, or even weapons. 
# -	Material:
	These goods are generally not very useful by themselves, but used as a material in crafting, construction,
	or the like. They generally do not expire, and basic materials are usually traded in bulk.
# -	Mining: 
	Basic mining goods are usually gathered in large quantities through mines or quarries, through physically demanding
	labour, often requiring the use of pickaxes or heavier tools to gather efficiently. The higher end mining goods,
	in contrast, usually still require brawn, but also brain and often specialised tools, to not just gather efficiently,
	but ensure that the workers gathering it survive that endeavour.
	The priority of the latter point usually depends on the employer. The intelligent of which will at least weigh the
	cost of losing a well-trained worker, versus the increased extraction rate of avoiding cowardly and wholly unnecessary
	"safety measures". 
# -	Refined:
	Refined goods are often the result of refining/processing one or more basic goods into a more useful items for some production trees.
	Generally speaking, the more refined a good is, the higher its value, reflecting the labour and material cost in producing it.
	Although more stages of refinement generally increase the value of a good, it does also increase their niche usage.
	This effectively means that they cannot be sold for profit, if at all, in most places, save for those which 
	specifically require that good. 
	An example: Iron ingots are needed in most places, but iron gears are usually only in-demand in places with high development,
	for use in for example manufacturies or advanced infrastructure (like heavy drawbridges).
# - Meat:
	Meat goods are usually gained from butchering animals. They are usually edible, though not particularly tasty uncooked.
	Most meat goods spoil relatively quickly, unless they are preserved, either through drying or another method.
	They count as food for most chintabeings, though most prefer to cook it before consumption.
	Some chintabeings avoid the consumption of meat, which is useful to know before one brings a caravan packed with meat
	to a town consisting of vegetarians.

Iron ingot:
	Traits:
		Material,
		Crafts,
		Refined,
	Description:
		Iron ingots or other semi-standardised mostly uniform iron items are transported around the world to be smelted
		down for various uses. It is an essential good for most places with enough wealth to afford them, 
		as they not only are necessary for crafting cost-effective durable tools, but also enable the construction or
		crafting of many things for which wood or stone cannot be used effectively.
