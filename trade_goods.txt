# This document will contain all trade goods in the game
# It also contains more information about the trade goods, such as their traits.
# Full trait list, and explanation:
# -	Basic: means that the good is at the first stage of production trees, assigned to all goods which are extracted from farms,
	mines, etc. The price of this good will determine the prices of more advanced goods in the area, but huge fluctuations
	in its availability will often lead to the destruction of livelihoods, famines, and other unpleasant events.
	Then again, great crises may lead to even greater profits...
# -	Food: means that the good is edible for most chintabeings. A minimum local production or import of goods with this trait,
	are necessary to ensure that this location does not starve.
# -	Grain: means that the good is a type of grain, which can be important for different reasons,
	one of which include the desire for some cities to have a varied diet.
# -	Agriculture: refers to goods which are the results of agriculture
# -	Crafts: refers to goods which is used in, or part of, a significant amount of production chains which involve
	crafting something, whether it is for handicraft, tools for farming, or even weapons. 
# -	Material: These goods are generally not very useful by themselves, but used as a material in crafting, construction,
	or the like. They generally do not expire, and basic materials are usually traded in bulk.
# -	Mining: Basic mining goods are usually gathered in large quantities through mines or quarries, through physically demanding
	labour, often requiring the use of pickaxes or heavier tools to gather efficiently. The higher end mining goods,
	in contrast, usually still require brawn, but also brain and often specialised tools, to not just gather efficiently,
	but ensure that the workers gathering it survive that endeavour.
	The priority of the latter point usually depends on the employer. The intelligent of which will at least weigh the
	cost of losing a well-trained worker, versus the increased extraction rate of avoiding cowardly and wholly unnecessary
	"safety measures". 
	
	

Wheat
	Traits: 
		Basic
		Food
		Grain
		Agriculture
	Description: 
		Wheat is a staple grain in most nations with temperate environments. Although not a lucrative good for the average trader,
		those who control the flow of this good, will have power beyond what even profits can buy.

	Description:

Iron ore
	Traits:
		Basic,
		Crafts,
		Material,
		Mining
	Description:
		One of the most important resources in existence, used to create tools and weapons of iron, a respectably durable
		material. In some places, iron is further refined along with coal to create an even stronger alloy.

	Traits:
		Basic,
		Crafts,
		Material,
		Mining,
	Description:
		Whether gained as a result of a failed cooking attempt, or the hard labour of Chintabeings, coal is a useful

Marble:
	Traits:
		Basic,
		Construction,
		Crafts,
		Material,
		Mining,
	Description:
		Stone, but nicer, somehow. Also more difficult to extract without breaking it into too small pieces. Thus this the
		flow of marble has historically been dominated by the Dwarves, as few others Chintabeings have the required strength
		and artisanry required to extract marble in great quantities.
		Much to the chagrin of several traders, who allege that Dwarven guilds are bad for business, and a detriment to all.
