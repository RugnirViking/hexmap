# Full trait list, and explanation:
# -	Basic: means that the good is at the first stage of production trees, assigned to all goods which are extracted from farms,
	mines, etc. The price of this good will determine the prices of more advanced goods in the area, but huge fluctuations
	in its availability will often lead to the destruction of livelihoods, famines, and other unpleasant events.
	Then again, great crises may lead to even greater profits...
# -	Food: means that the good is edible for most chintabeings. A minimum local production or import of goods with this trait,
	are necessary to ensure that this location does not starve.
# -	Grain: means that the good is a type of grain, which can be important for different reasons,
	one of which include the desire for some cities to have a varied diet.
# -	Agriculture: refers to goods which are the results of agriculture
# -	Crafts: refers to goods which is used in, or part of, a significant amount of production chains which involve
	crafting something, whether it is for handicraft, tools for farming, or even weapons. 
# -	Material: These goods are generally not very useful by themselves, but used as a material in crafting, construction,
	or the like. They generally do not expire, and basic materials are usually traded in bulk.
	

Wheat
	Traits: 
		Basic
		Food
		Grain
		Agriculture
	Description: 
		Wheat is a staple grain in most nations with temperate environments. Although not a lucrative good for the average trader,
		those who control the flow of this good, will have power beyond what even profits can buy.
